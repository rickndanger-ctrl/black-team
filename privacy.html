Privacy of this website

This website itself sets no cookies, has no analytics, and loads nothing from other sites: its fonts, images and script are served from the same place as the page. Its only links go to GitHub, where you choose whether to follow them.

The privacy rules for the Black Team Agent app are a separate matter. They are in the repository, in docs/PRIVACY.md, and they describe what the app does on your computer and what can leave it.
